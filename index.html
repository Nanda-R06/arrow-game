How to Play 🏹

Clear every arrow from the grid!

🎯 Objective: Release every arrow until the board is completely empty.


➡️ Flight Path: When tapped, an arrow attempts to fly straight in the direction it points (↑, ↓, ←, → or diagonals).


💥 Collisions: If another arrow or stone obstacle blocks its direct path, it will collide and bounce back!


🧠 Order Matters: Untangle the outermost unblocked arrows first to clear paths for arrows trapped in the center.


⭐ Combos & Stars: Consecutive escapes build up combos and higher scores. Complete with 0 mistakes for 3 Stars!
